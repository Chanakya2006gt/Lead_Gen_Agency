## 4. Architectural Decision Log: Evidence-Driven Lead Qualification & "Not Your Client" Detection (AD-035)

### Context & Paradigm Shift
Traditional lead generation tools operate on the brittle assumption:
$$\text{Missing Feature} \rightarrow \text{Agency Can Build Feature} \rightarrow \text{Sell Feature} \rightarrow \text{Generate Outreach}$$

This produced false positives where software products (like TRELIO) were flagged for missing 1-tap dental consultation widgets or WhatsApp phone triggers.

### Architectural Invariant Enforced
$$\text{Observation} \rightarrow \text{Business Context} \rightarrow \text{Relevance} \rightarrow \text{Business Problem} \rightarrow \text{Commercial Opportunity} \rightarrow \text{Agency Fit} \rightarrow \text{Qualification} \rightarrow \text{Outreach}$$

### Components Introduced & Integrated
1. **`CustomerJourneyDetector`**: Detects verified acquisition workflows (`DISCOVER_SIGN_UP`, `DISCOVER_REQUEST_DEMO`, `DISCOVER_REQUEST_QUOTE`, `DISCOVER_BOOK`, `DISCOVER_CALL`, `DISCOVER_WHATSAPP`, `DISCOVER_PURCHASE`, `DISCOVER_VISIT_LOCATION`, `UNKNOWN`).
2. **`QualificationEngine`**:
   - Computes independent `businessQualityScore` (0–100) vs `opportunityFitScore` (0–100).
   - Introduces first-class `LeadDisposition`: `PURSUE`, `LOW_OPPORTUNITY`, `NOT_A_FIT`, `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`.
   - Explicitly rejects SaaS entities, industrial exporters, and clean operational sites with `NOT_A_FIT` (`"Good business, but no sufficiently evidenced agency opportunity"`).
3. **Hard Outreach Boundary**:
   - When `disposition === "NOT_A_FIT"` or `"INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE"` or `"LOW_OPPORTUNITY"` $\rightarrow$ `outreachAllowed = false`.
   - Disables outreach generation and renders prominent `[OUTREACH GATED — NOT A FIT]` explanation badges in the UI.
4. **Permanent Adversarial Suite (`LeadQualificationAndNotYourClient.test.ts`)**:
   - 7 test scenarios verifying TRELIO, industrial manufacturers, unknown entities, and high-volume clinics.
   - 110 total Vitest tests passing cleanly.
